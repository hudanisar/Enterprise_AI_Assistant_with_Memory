# Fictional Acme Company Policy (sample data)

This is fictional sample data for testing the Enterprise AI Assistant.

## Working hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00 local time, Monday through Friday.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 20 paid annual-leave days per calendar year. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance when practical.

## Remote work
Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Employees must remain reachable during core collaboration hours.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick-leave days annually. Notify a manager as soon as possible.
